Printing Tips

  • Print at 100% / Actual Size (turn off "fit to page") on US Letter paper.
  • Cards: cardstock feels great; regular paper works fine for a desk drop.
  • Badge tags: a 1.6” square punch is the fast way — or just cut on the dashed frame.
  • Banner: cardstock holds up better on string; a normal paper tab-tape fold works too.
  • Color printer ideal, but every design also reads fine in grayscale.
